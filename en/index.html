FAQ

Where is my data? On your device. Habilo has no accounts or servers. If you want, you can turn on a daily iCloud backup in Settings.

How do I move my habits to another iPhone? Turn on iCloud backup in Settings. On the new iPhone, with the same iCloud account, open Habilo and choose “Restore from iCloud” in Settings. You can also export a backup and import it.

What’s included for free? You can track 3 habits with no time limit. Habilo Pro adds unlimited habits, charts, widgets and more reminders.

How does the free trial work? The yearly plan includes 14 days free if you haven’t used the trial before. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription starts. Apple shows you the price before you confirm.

How do I cancel my subscription? In your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Habilo.
