Privacy Policy

Habilo · Updated November 2, 2026

Habilo has no accounts or servers of its own. Your habits, days, notes and settings are stored on your device. We never receive them and can’t see them.

iCloud backup. It’s optional and stays off until you turn it on. When it’s on, Habilo saves one backup a day to your private iCloud account, which Apple manages. We can’t access it. Turning it off in Settings deletes the backups from iCloud.

Backups and images. The backups you export and the images you share only leave your device when you choose.

Third parties. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking, and we don’t share data with anyone.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Habilo only receives confirmation that you have Pro, with no payment details.

Permissions. They’re optional. Notifications are used for your reminders, and the photo library only to save the images you choose to save.

Deleting your data. “Delete all my data” in Settings removes it from your device and from iCloud. If you only delete the app, your data disappears from the device, but the iCloud backup stays in your account until you remove it from your iPhone’s iCloud settings.

Questions about your privacy? Email us at anisu.apps@gmail.com.
